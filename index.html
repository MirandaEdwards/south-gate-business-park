Project Vision

Building Opportunity at a Gateway to Greater Sudbury

South Gate Business Park is a planned employment and commercial development located at the intersection of Highway 17 and Long Lake Road, one of the principal gateways into the City of Greater Sudbury.

The project is intended to create a high-quality business environment that supports investment, employment growth, commercial services, and future economic development, while enhancing this important gateway to the city.

Development will occur over time and remains subject to municipal planning approvals, technical review, agency input, and applicable regulatory requirements.

Questions or Concerns?

Current Site Activities

Preparing for Future Development

As South Gate Business Park moves through the planning and development process, site preparation activities are underway to support future infrastructure and development.

Work occurring within designated areas of the property may include grading, drilling, blasting, and material management associated with future road construction and site servicing. These activities represent an important step in preparing the lands for future business and employment opportunities.

Activities are conducted in accordance with applicable approvals, environmental requirements, and industry best practices.

Our Commitment

Responsible development and open communication

Teranorth is committed to advancing South Gate Business Park in a responsible and transparent manner.

As both the developer and contractor for current site preparation activities, Teranorth recognizes the importance of communicating clearly with nearby residents, responding to concerns, and implementing practical mitigation measures to reduce impacts on neighbouring properties and the surrounding community.

Resident feedback, technical review, and regulatory requirements will continue to inform how construction activities and project communications are managed as the development progresses.
