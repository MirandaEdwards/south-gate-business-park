Current Site Activities

Site preparation and future road construction support

Grading, drilling, and blasting activities are occurring on portions of the property to support future road construction and site preparation. Material generated during blasting operations is being reused where practical for future road construction and site grading, helping reduce waste and reduce the need for imported materials.

As road construction and future development progress, major site preparation activities are expected to decrease.

Current status

  • Planning review stage
  • Technical and agency review ongoing
  • Site preparation activities underway
  • Future development subject to approvals
