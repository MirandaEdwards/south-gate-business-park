FAQ

Common Questions

Answers to common resident questions about construction, blasting, dust, traffic, and approvals.

What is being proposed?

South Gate Business Park is a planned business and employment area intended to support future investment, employment opportunities, and economic growth while remaining subject to municipal approvals, technical studies, and regulatory review.

Why is work happening on the property now?

Current grading, drilling, and blasting activities support future road construction and site preparation. Material generated onsite is being reused where practical for future road construction and site grading.

Is the project fully approved?

The project is in the planning review stage and has not received final approval. Several steps remain, including Official Plan Amendment review, Zoning By-law Amendment review, technical and agency review, and future approvals if planning approvals are granted.

Why is blasting required?

Blasting is used where rock excavation is required to support future road construction and site preparation.

How is vibration monitored?

Independent seismographs may be placed at nearby locations to record ground vibration and air overpressure during blasting.

Did the August 4, 2026 blast exceed the residential guideline noted in the monitoring report?

The monitoring report recorded peak particle velocities of 3.175 mm/s and 9.398 mm/s at the monitored locations. The report notes an OPSS 120 recommended guideline of 50 mm/s at the closest residence observation point.

What is being done about dust?

Teranorth is updating its Dust Mitigation and Response Protocol with additional dust suppression, enhanced monitoring, improved stop-work and escalation procedures, and a formal communication protocol for resident concerns.

Who can residents contact?

Residents can contact Miranda Edwards at Teranorth or Sarah Vereault at J.L. Richards using the contact information listed on the Contact page.
