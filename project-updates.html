Project Updates

News and Project Information

Updates for residents and stakeholders as construction and planning review progress.

August 2026

Dust mitigation and response protocol update

Following resident feedback regarding dust associated with blasting activities, Teranorth completed an investigation and is updating its Dust Mitigation and Response Protocol to include additional dust suppression measures, enhanced monitoring requirements, clearer stop-work and escalation triggers, and a communication protocol to improve response and follow-up.

August 2026

Independent vibration monitoring summary

Independent vibration and air overpressure monitoring was completed at nearby residences. The monitoring confirmed that blast vibration levels were below the Ontario recommended residential blasting guideline referenced in the monitoring report.

Ongoing

Planning and review process

The project remains subject to municipal approvals, technical studies, environmental review, agency review, and future site-specific approvals before future buildings or businesses can proceed.
