Project Overview

About South Gate Business Park

A planned business and employment area intended to support future investment, employment opportunities, and economic growth in Greater Sudbury.

About the proposal

Teranorth Construction & Engineering Limited is proposing South Gate Business Park, a planned business and employment area intended to support future investment, employment opportunities, and economic growth within the community.

The property is designated for Mixed-Use Commercial development and identified as a Special Concept Area, supporting a range of commercial, office, service, and employment-related uses. The vision is to create a professionally planned business park that accommodates future growth while respecting the surrounding area and meeting municipal planning requirements.

Development would occur over time and remains subject to municipal approvals, technical studies, and regulatory review.

Current site activities

Grading, drilling, and blasting activities are occurring on portions of the property. These activities are being completed to support future road construction and prepare the site for development.

Material generated during blasting operations is being reused where practical for future road construction and site grading, helping reduce waste and the need for imported materials.

Planning, environmental, and technical review

The project is currently in the planning review stage and has not received final approval. Studies are being completed to understand existing conditions and identify mitigation measures required to support future development.

Environmental information and technical studies will be reviewed by municipal staff and applicable regulatory agencies as part of the approval process.

Transportation and servicing

Traffic, water, and wastewater servicing requirements are being evaluated as part of the planning review process. Required infrastructure improvements will be identified through the review and incorporated into future development plans.

Planning steps

  1. Official Plan Amendment review
  2. Zoning By-law Amendment review
  3. Technical and agency review
  4. Future subdivision approval, if planning approvals are granted
  5. Future site-specific approvals for buildings and businesses
