Project Overview

About South Gate Business Park

A planned employment and commercial development at one of Greater Sudbury’s principal gateway locations.

South Gate Business Park

South Gate Business Park is a planned employment and commercial development located at the intersection of Highway 17 and Long Lake Road, one of the principal gateways into the City of Greater Sudbury.

The project is intended to create a high-quality business environment that supports investment, employment growth, commercial services, and future economic development while enhancing this important gateway to the city.

Development will occur over time and remains subject to municipal planning approvals, technical review, agency input, and applicable regulatory requirements.

Project Vision

The vision for South Gate Business Park is to create a professionally planned business and employment destination that supports long-term economic growth within Greater Sudbury.

The project is intended to accommodate a range of employment, commercial, and business-supporting uses that contribute to investment, job creation, and economic activity while complementing the surrounding area.

Through thoughtful planning and infrastructure investment, South Gate Business Park is envisioned as a gateway development that supports future growth opportunities for the community.

A Strategic Gateway Location

Situated at the intersection of Highway 17 and Long Lake Road, South Gate Business Park occupies a highly visible and accessible location within Greater Sudbury.

Its proximity to major transportation corridors, established commercial areas, and supporting infrastructure positions the site to support future employment and economic development opportunities while strengthening an important entrance to the city.

Current Project Status

South Gate Business Park is currently progressing through the planning, technical review, and site preparation stages necessary to support future development.

Concurrent planning, engineering, and environmental review activities are underway to evaluate future infrastructure requirements, establish appropriate development standards, and support future phases of development.

Site preparation activities are also occurring within designated areas of the property to support future infrastructure and development readiness.

Planning and Approval Process

The project remains subject to a comprehensive planning and review process that includes municipal planning review, technical studies, agency input, environmental review, subdivision approvals where required, and future site-specific development approvals.

This review process helps ensure future development is appropriately planned, supported by necessary infrastructure, and aligned with applicable planning policies and regulations.

Looking Ahead

South Gate Business Park is being advanced as a long-term employment and commercial development opportunity within Greater Sudbury.

Future development is anticipated to occur in phases and will continue to be subject to required approvals, technical review, and regulatory requirements as the project progresses.
