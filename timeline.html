Project Status & Timeline

Project Progress

An overview of the project’s anticipated progression and current status.

Current Stage | 2026

Planning Review & Approvals

Status: In Progress

Municipal planning applications and technical review are currently underway. Planning approvals are anticipated to be obtained in 2026, subject to municipal review and decision-making processes.

Site preparation activities are ongoing within designated areas of the property to support future development readiness.

Next Stage | 2027

Detailed Design & Subdivision Approvals

Status: Future Stage

Following planning approvals, the project is anticipated to advance into detailed engineering and infrastructure design.

Subdivision approvals, servicing design, transportation considerations, and supporting technical requirements will continue to be refined as the project progresses. Site preparation activities may continue as required to support future infrastructure and development planning.

Future Stage | 2028

Subdivision Infrastructure Construction

Status: Future Stage

Subject to approvals and design completion, subdivision infrastructure construction is anticipated to begin in 2028.

This phase is intended to establish the roads, services, and supporting infrastructure necessary to facilitate future development within South Gate Business Park and create development-ready employment and commercial lands.

Development of individual lots is expected to occur gradually over time as lands are purchased and advanced through site-specific planning, design, approval, and construction processes.

While subdivision infrastructure construction is anticipated to commence in 2028, the build-out of South Gate Business Park is expected to occur over a number of years and will be influenced by market demand, investment opportunities, and future development approvals.

Long-Term Vision

Future Business & Employment Destination

South Gate Business Park is envisioned as a professionally planned employment and commercial development that supports investment, job creation, and economic growth in Greater Sudbury.

Over time, the development is intended to evolve into a gateway business destination that contributes to the City’s long-term economic development objectives.
