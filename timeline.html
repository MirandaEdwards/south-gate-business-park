Project Timeline

Planning and Review Process

A simplified overview of the major planning and approval steps for South Gate Business Park.

Current

Official Plan and Zoning Review

The City is reviewing applications to determine whether the proposed land use is appropriate and consistent with long-term planning objectives.

Next

Technical and Agency Review

Traffic, environmental, stormwater, servicing, MTO, and Conservation Sudbury review continues through the application process.

Future

Future Subdivision Approval

If planning approvals are granted, future subdivision review would establish road layout, lot configuration, servicing requirements, and conditions.

Before Construction

Site-Specific Development Review

Future businesses may require additional approvals, site plans, permits, and technical review before construction can occur.

Important note

The project is still in the planning review stage. Future details will be refined through technical review, agency comments, and conditions of approval.
