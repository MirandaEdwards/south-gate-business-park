Project Information & FAQ

Project Information and Frequently Asked Questions

Information about site preparation, blasting, environmental considerations, community considerations, and common questions.

Site Preparation Activities

South Gate Business Park is progressing through the planning and development process to support future employment, commercial, and economic development opportunities within Greater Sudbury.

To support future development, site preparation activities are occurring within designated areas of the property. These activities help prepare the lands for future infrastructure, servicing, and development and are intended to support future phases of planning, design, and construction.

As the project advances, site activities will continue to evolve in response to planning requirements, technical review, regulatory requirements, and development needs.

All project activities are undertaken in accordance with applicable approvals, regulatory requirements, and industry practices.

Blasting Activities

Certain site preparation activities may require controlled blasting operations where rock excavation is necessary to support future development.

Where blasting is required, activities are completed by qualified contractors using established procedures intended to support safe, effective, and responsible operations. Monitoring and assessment activities may be undertaken, where appropriate, to support compliance with applicable standards, project requirements, and regulatory obligations.

Blasting activities form one component of the broader site preparation program and are planned and managed as part of the overall development process.

Dust Management & Environmental Considerations

South Gate Business Park is being advanced with a commitment to responsible environmental management and good construction practices.

Site preparation activities may generate temporary dust during certain operations and under certain weather conditions. A variety of mitigation measures may be implemented as appropriate to site conditions, operational requirements, and environmental considerations.

Environmental planning, review, and management continue throughout the planning and development process. Environmental considerations are incorporated into project planning and implementation to support compliance with applicable requirements, approvals, and commitments.

As development progresses, the project team will continue to evaluate and implement reasonable measures intended to support environmental stewardship and responsible site management.

Community Considerations

As planning, site preparation, and development activities progress, nearby residents and stakeholders may periodically observe project-related activity occurring on the property.

Temporary impacts associated with development activities can vary depending on project stage, site conditions, and the nature of activities being undertaken. The project team continues to evaluate and implement reasonable measures intended to minimize impacts where practical and appropriate while supporting the advancement of the project.

Questions, comments, and concerns are welcomed and can be directed to the project team through the Contact page.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is South Gate Business Park?

South Gate Business Park is a planned employment and commercial development located at the intersection of Highway 17 and Long Lake Road, one of the principal gateways into the City of Greater Sudbury. The project is intended to support business investment, employment growth, commercial opportunities, and long-term economic development while contributing to the continued growth of the community.

What stage is the project currently in?

South Gate Business Park is currently advancing through planning review, technical review, and site preparation activities that support future development. Please refer to the Project Status & Timeline page for current milestones.

Why are site preparation activities occurring?

Site preparation activities support future planning, infrastructure, servicing, and development requirements. These activities represent an important step in preparing the lands for future development and investment opportunities.

Why is blasting occurring?

Blasting may be required where rock excavation is necessary to support future development activities. Where blasting is undertaken, it is completed using established procedures and practices intended to support safe and responsible operations.

How are dust and environmental considerations managed?

Environmental considerations are incorporated throughout the planning, review, design, and development process. Dust management, environmental review, and site management activities are undertaken as appropriate to support compliance with applicable approvals, requirements, and project commitments.

Will South Gate Business Park create employment opportunities?

The long-term vision for South Gate Business Park is to create a high-quality employment and commercial destination that supports business investment, employment growth, and economic development within Greater Sudbury.

Who is leading the project?

South Gate Business Park is being advanced by Teranorth Construction & Engineering Limited, serving as both the developer and contractor for the project. J.L. Richards & Associates Limited is providing planning, engineering, and detailed design consulting services in support of the project.
