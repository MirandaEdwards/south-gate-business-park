Dust & Environment

Environmental Protection and Dust Management

How dust-generating work is planned, monitored, and addressed.

01

Prevent

Review weather, wind direction, and site conditions. Use water trucks, pre-wetting, haul road controls, and source controls where required.

02

Monitor

Observe visible dust, property boundaries, public roads, and sensitive receptors during dust-sensitive work.

03

Respond

Increase controls, reduce vehicle speeds, modify work, or suspend affected activities if dust approaches or crosses the property boundary.

04

Report

Assess complaints, off-site migration, potential adverse effects, and regulatory notification requirements. Document the decision and follow-up.

Additional mitigation measures

  • Enhanced dust suppression before and after blasting
  • Additional monitoring during dust-sensitive activities
  • Improved stop-work and escalation procedures
  • Formal communication protocol for resident concerns
  • Incident documentation and follow-up review

When to contact us

Please contact the project team if dust, vibration, noise, traffic, or other construction-related concerns are observed. Include the approximate time, location, nature of the concern, and any photos if available.
